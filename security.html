Your money and data are protected.

Revora handles ad money and customer orders, so we build safeguards into every step.

🔐

PIN for every money move

Sending money needs the owner or admin's transfer PIN. The PIN goes straight to Revora and is never typed into a chat with an AI app.

🏢

Company isolation

Every record belongs to one company and is locked to it in the database. One company can never see another's data.

👥

Role-based access

Owners, admins, media buyers, customer care and delivery agents each see only what their job needs.

🔑

Your Meta access stays yours

You choose which ad accounts, Pages and numbers to share, and you can remove access any time. Access tokens live on our servers, never in your browser.

💳

Regulated payments

Card and transfer payments run through Paystack. Revora is not a bank and does not store card details.

⏸️

Spending limits that protect you

On ad accounts bought from Revora, ads can only spend what is in your wallet and pause when it is empty.

You can disconnect AI apps any time in Settings, then AI apps. To delete your data, see data deletion. Read our privacy policy and terms.
