# Images for the Revora landing pages (/start.html, /ad-creation.html, /ad-improvement.html)

Put the files in this folder (images/landing/) with exactly these names. A slot with no file hides itself (lesson pictures) or shows a blue gradient (hero and intro pictures).

## Style (use for every picture)
Clean, bright, highly visual. One clear subject, lots of white space, soft natural light, modern lifestyle photography of real-looking Nigerian people and products. Calm backgrounds (white, pale blue, soft grey). NO text, NO logos, NO readable screens: the words and small labels are added on the page itself, so they stay sharp and spelled right.
Sizes: lesson pictures 1600x1000 (16:10), hero tiles 1000x1250 (4:5), intros 1600x1000. JPG, under 300 KB each (squoosh.app).
