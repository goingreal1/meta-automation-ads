# Autopilot hard stop: how end_time and the currency reach Meta (read from code)

1. `ai-team` `approveLaunch` computes `end = start + run_days*24h` (`run_days = floor(total / daily)`) and calls mcp-ads `plan_campaign` with `start_time`, `end_time` and `budget_currency` (the ad account's currency).
2. `mcp-plan` `plan_campaign` wrapper checks `budget_currency` equals the account's currency (`currencyMismatch`; a mismatch refuses the plan), then writes the real budgets and `plan.currency` onto the plan (`ai_plans.plan`).
3. `mcp-ads` `plan_campaign` validates both times (offset required, start 15 min to 6 months ahead, end at least 1 h after start) and saves `scheduled_for` and `campaign_end_date` on `ai_plans.plan`.
4. `launch_plan` -> `ai-chat approve_plan` stages the ads (`uploaded_by = 'ai_chat'`) and calls `ai-auto-launch-tests`.
5. `ai-auto-launch-tests launchAdSetGroup` finds the plan (`ai_plans`, status `launching`, same campaign name), takes `startTime`/`endTime` from it, refuses a plan whose currency differs from the account's, and `buildAdSetPayload` sends `start_time` and `end_time` on EVERY ad set it creates (`if (opts.endTime) payload.end_time = ...`). Budgets go as `daily_budget = round(budget * 100)` in the account's currency.

So the end date IS applied on Meta, per ad set. What it cannot do: Meta may spend up to ~25% above a day's budget on a single day, so `days x daily` is an average, not a ceiling. Hence the safety net in the watch step (`hardStopCheck` in `_shared/autopilot_watch.ts`): on every look (about every 8 minutes) the run's whole-life spend (Meta, lifetime, the run's own campaign) is compared with the stop amount. Half an hour of normal spending BEFORE the amount, the campaign is paused with mcp-ads `set_status` (-> ads-manager `pauseOrResume`, the same call the auto-off cron makes), the log says so, the owner is notified and the run is closed. If Meta refuses the pause the run keeps trying every look and the owner is told to pause by hand.
Remaining limit: Meta's own reporting lags by minutes, and the check runs every ~8 minutes, so the total can still pass the stop by a few minutes of spend; the early margin is sized for that.
