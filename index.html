Most teams run ads in one tab, track orders in a spreadsheet, confirm customers by hand, and reconcile delivery and cash days later — if at all.

Revora is the one place that actually connects them — so a naira spent on an ad can be traced all the way to a naira collected on delivery.

One system, five jobs

Not just another CRM

Built around how a performance commerce team actually operates — marketing, sales, operations, and finance, reading from the same numbers.

Marketing

  • Every campaign, ad set, and ad synced live from Meta
  • Automatic kill / scale decisions on CTR and CPA

Sales & CRM

  • Orders confirmed by AI before a rider is dispatched
  • A WhatsApp number that sells and answers questions

Operations

  • Riders see only their own assignments and payment status
  • Media buyer leaderboard, ranked by real revenue
