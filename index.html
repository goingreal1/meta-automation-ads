Platform

Everything between a click and cash in hand

Most teams stitch this together from Meta Ads Manager, a spreadsheet, a WhatsApp number, and a bank app. Revora is the one place that actually connects them.

📣

Live campaign structure

Every campaign, ad set, and ad synced straight from Meta — real creative, copy, and performance, drilled down the way Ads Manager shows it, not a flattened spreadsheet.

🤖

AI order confirmation

New orders get an automatic confirmation call before anything ships — catching fake numbers and no-shows before a rider is ever dispatched.

💬

WhatsApp selling

A connected WhatsApp number that talks to customers, answers product questions, and takes orders directly inside the chat.

🛵

Delivery & collections

Every rider sees their own assignments and exactly which customers already paid online versus who still owes on delivery — no more guessing or double-collecting.

💰

Real funding wallet

Admins fund a real wallet from their own bank, buyers request what they need for ad spend, and every transfer is tracked back to who approved it.

📊

Real ROAS, not guesswork

ROAS calculated off confirmed payments — not just orders placed — so scaling decisions are based on cash that actually landed.
