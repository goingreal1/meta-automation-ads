Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

Revora ("we", "us") provides a dashboard for online businesses and media buyers in Nigeria: it manages orders, deliveries, websites, ads and WhatsApp conversations in one place. This policy explains what data we handle, why, and the choices you have. We follow the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

1. Who is who

  • Our customers (businesses and their media buyers, admins, customer-care staff and delivery agents) create accounts and use Revora. For their own account data we are the controller.
  • Our customers' customers (people who order, message or are called by a business) are data subjects of that business. For that data we act as a processor: the business decides what to do with it and we process it on their instructions.

3. Meta (Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp) permissions

When you choose "Continue with Facebook" or "Set up WhatsApp", Meta shows exactly what you are sharing and you pick the ad accounts, pages and numbers. We use that access only to:

  • read your ad spend, results and balance (ads_read);
  • create, pause, resume and edit the campaigns and ads you ask for, and send Purchase events for your confirmed orders to your own dataset (ads_management);
  • list your ad accounts, pages and WhatsApp accounts so you can choose (business_management, pages_show_list, pages_read_engagement);
  • create or connect your WhatsApp number and send and receive messages with your customers on it (whatsapp_business_management, whatsapp_business_messaging).

We do not sell Meta data, use it for advertising to you, or share it with anyone except the service providers below. You can remove access at any time in Revora (Settings) or in Facebook (Settings → Business Integrations).

4. Who we share data with

Only providers that help us run the service, under contract: Supabase (database and login), Vercel (hosting), Meta Platforms (ads and WhatsApp), Paystack (payments), OpenAI (AI features such as the website builder and assistant, only the text needed for the request), and Telnyx (phone calls, if enabled). We may disclose data when the law requires it.

5. How long we keep it

While your account is active. After you delete your account or ask us to delete data, we remove it within 30 days, except records we must keep by law (for example payment records) and encrypted backups, which expire within 90 days.

6. Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Access is limited by role and by company, so one business cannot see another's data. Access tokens from Meta are stored on our servers and are never shown in the browser. No system is perfectly secure; if there is a breach that affects you we will tell you and the regulator as the law requires.

7. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete your data, object to some processing, or withdraw consent. Customers of a business should first contact that business; we will help the business respond. To delete your data see Delete your data. You may also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

8. Children

Revora is for businesses and is not meant for anyone under 18.
