Step 1 of 4

Buy your ad account.

Open Revora, go to Settings, then Ad accounts, and find Buy a Meta ad account from Revora. Give it a name, tap Buy, and it is yours. It is paid from your Revora wallet, and the price (₦3,000 per account) is shown before you confirm.

Step 3 of 4

Link your Page and launch.

Ads run as a Facebook Page. Share your Page with Revora once, paste its ID, and your ad account is ready to launch. Then plan and launch your ad from the dashboard, or by chatting with ChatGPT or Claude.

Share Page with RevoraPage linked
  1. In Meta Business Settings, open Pages, then Add partner, and enter Revora's Business ID (shown in the Pages tab).
  2. Give permission to create ads.
  3. In Revora, open Settings, then Pages, tap Link Page and paste your Page ID.
  4. Launch. Your ad is live.
Stay in controlWhen your wallet runs out, Revora pauses your running ads so you never spend what you have not paid.
