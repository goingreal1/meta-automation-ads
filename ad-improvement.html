Lesson 1 of 7

Track performance against your goal.

Clicks and likes do not pay your bills. Revora puts the numbers that matter next to each other: what you spent, what each result cost, how many orders came in, and how many of them were actually delivered and paid for.

  • Spend and cost per result for each ad account, ad set and ad.
  • Orders and their status, from pending to delivered.
  • Real ROAS: the money you earned from delivered orders divided by what you spent. This is the honest number.

Pick the metric that fits your goal.

An ad should be judged by the result it was made for. Judging a website ad by message cost, or the other way round, leads to wrong decisions, so Revora keeps them apart.

💬

WhatsApp ads

🛒

Website ads

Goal: get orders on your sales page.
Judged on: cost per purchase.

Lesson 3 of 7

Let auto-kill protect your budget.

You set your own limit once. Revora then checks your live ads every few minutes, all day, even while you sleep.

  1. You set a rule for each kind of ad: the most you will pay for one result, and how much it must spend, and for how many hours, before it is judged. This stops good ads being killed too early.
  2. Revora checks every ad. An ad is flagged if it spent that much with no results at all, or if each result costs more than your limit.
  3. Your choice of action. With auto-pause on, Revora pauses the ad for you and tells you why. With it off, you get a "Kill this ad?" message and approve with one tap.
  4. Everything is recorded. Each pause is logged with the numbers behind it, so you can see what happened and why.

Winners are never stoppedThe rule only targets ads that cost too much or bring nothing. You decide whether to scale the good ones.
Lesson 4 of 7

Get advice on what to kill, keep or scale.

Ask the AI to review your ads like a senior media buyer. It explains the answer in plain words, not a wall of numbers, and says what to do next. It does not change anything until you say yes.

🛑

Kill

Ads that spent and brought nothing, or cost far more than your limit. Relaunch a bad ad as a fresh copy to give the idea one more chance.

👀

Keep

Ads that are still learning, or not spent enough to judge. Give them time.

📈

Scale

Ads with strong results. Raise the budget by about 20 percent at a time, or duplicate the winner so it keeps running.

Try sayingWhich of my ads should I kill, keep or scale? Do not change anything yet.
Lesson 5 of 7

How Revora helps you sell more and spend less.

  • Stop waste early. Ads that bring nothing are paused by your own rule, not after a week of lost money.
  • Back the winners. You see which ads bring real, delivered orders, so the extra budget goes where it works.
  • See the real return. Real ROAS counts delivered and paid orders, not just clicks.

Lesson 6 of 7

Follow every order to delivery.

An ad only pays when the goods are delivered and paid for. Revora handles the steps in between.

🏍️

Your rider is alerted

When an order is confirmed, Revora offers it to a delivery agent in the right area. The rider gets a notification on their phone and accepts or declines in their "My deliveries" screen. If no one answers in a few minutes, it is offered to the next rider.

💳

Payment marks it delivered

The customer pays into your account number. Revora matches the transfer to the order, using the order's code in the transfer or the sender's name and exact amount, and marks the order delivered.

The customer then gets a WhatsApp message that their payment arrived, and the rider is told to go ahead with delivery. Part payments and transfers that cannot be matched wait for a person to check, so nothing is marked delivered by mistake.

Lesson 7 of 7

Make better decisions with every order.

Every order you deliver teaches you something. Revora keeps the results, so your next ad starts smarter than the last one.

  • Compare ad sets to see which audience gives the cheapest purchases.
  • Re-use your best copy and pictures on new products.
  • Check each day's brief to see spend, orders, low balances and what needs you first.
