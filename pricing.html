Questions

Good to know.

How does the free trial work?

Create your company and use every feature for 7 days with no credit card. To continue, pick a plan. If you don't, you can export or delete your data.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. You keep access for the month you paid for. See the refund policy for mistakes and undelivered service.

Do I need my own ad account?

No. You can connect one you already have, or buy a ready one from Revora.

Do I need my own website domain?

To run ads to a page you build in Revora, connect your own domain. The free preview link is only for building and checking.
