Questions we hear most.

Who is Revora for?

Online sellers, media buyers and their teams in Nigeria who sell through WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook or a website.

Does Revora run my Facebook and Instagram ads?

Revora connects to your own Meta ad account. You see your results, and you can plan, launch, pause and scale ads from the dashboard or by chat. You always approve before money is spent.

Is my money safe?

Sending money needs your own transfer PIN, typed in a private form that is never shown to the AI. Each transfer gets a receipt, and limits apply per transfer and per day.

Can my team use it too?

Yes. Owners, admins, media buyers, customer care and delivery riders each see only what they need, and one business never sees another's data.

Can I use it from ChatGPT or Claude?

Yes. Connect Revora once and ask for what you need in plain words. You can disconnect it any time in Revora under Settings, then AI apps.
