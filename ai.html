How to start

Connect it in about 3 minutes.

You connect Revora to ChatGPT or Claude one time. After that, you just chat.

Use the website, not the phone app. Open ChatGPT or Claude in your browser to connect. When Revora has its own app, you will be able to connect from the app too.

● ChatGPT

  1. Create your free Revora account.
  2. Open ChatGPT on the web and log in.
  3. Go to Settings, then Plugins.
  4. Tap the + to add one, then choose Connect MCP.
  5. Name it Revora. Paste the link below. Leave it on OAuth.
  6. Log in with your Revora account and tap Allow.

● Claude

  1. Create your free Revora account.
  2. Open Claude on the web and log in.
  3. Go to Settings, then Connectors.
  4. Choose Add custom connector.
  5. Name it Revora and paste the link below.
  6. Log in with your Revora account and tap Allow.
https://revoracrm.app/mcp

Then say: "Plan my first campaign." Menu names can change a little. See the full connector guide if you get stuck.
