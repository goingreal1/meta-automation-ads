Create your first ad.

Good ads start with a clear plan. Whether you sell your own products or run ads for others, in nine short lessons you will see how to plan your ad, make the pictures and the words, send people to WhatsApp or to a sales page, and launch it, all by chatting with ChatGPT or Claude through Revora. You approve every step, and nothing goes live or costs money until you say yes.

Get started with Revora
Lesson 1 of 9

Connect ChatGPT or Claude.

Revora works inside the chat app you already use. You connect it once, sign in with your Revora login, and from then on you can ask for what you need in plain words. It acts only as you, with your own permissions.

  1. Create your Revora account and connect your Meta ad account.
  2. In ChatGPT or Claude, open Settings, then Connectors, and add Revora with the address shown on the connector page.
  3. Sign in with your Revora login and tap Allow.
Try sayingWho am I connected as, and what can you help me with?
You stay in controlYou can disconnect the app any time in Revora under Settings, then AI apps.
Lesson 2 of 9

Tell it what you sell.

An ad about the wrong product wastes money, so Revora makes sure the AI is sure which product the ad is for. Tell it the name, price, what it does and the top three benefits. It saves them on your product, and only uses facts you gave it.

  • Product name and price in naira
  • Where people order: on a website or by WhatsApp
  • A real photo of the product. This is what customers see.
Try sayingAdd a new product called "Herbal Tea", ₦8,500, customers order on WhatsApp.
Lesson 3 of 9

Choose where people go.

Every ad needs a destination. The choice decides how your ad is judged, so decide it before anything else.

💬

WhatsApp

People tap the ad and start a chat with you. Best when you like to talk to buyers first. The ad is judged on the cost of each message.

🛒

Sales page

People land on a page and order there. Best when the page can do the selling. The ad is judged on the cost of each purchase.

Try sayingI want people to order on a sales page, not WhatsApp.
Lesson 4 of 9

Build your sales page in the chat.

If people will order on a page, the AI interviews you first, like a good copywriter would. It tells you the page section by section, asks the few things it needs, makes the pictures, writes the story and shows it to you. Only then does it build the page.

  • You get a test link to look at and share for feedback.
  • Ask for changes in plain words and it edits the page.
  • It is published only after your clear yes.
Try sayingBuild a sales page for my Herbal Tea. Ask me what you need first.
Already have a page?Give it to the AI and it can import it, keeping your styles. Choosing WhatsApp? You can skip this lesson.
Lesson 5 of 9

Plan the campaign.

Now you decide who sees your ad and how much you spend. The AI turns your plain wishes into real Meta targeting, then writes the plan. Planning costs nothing and launches nothing.

🎯

Audience

Ages, gender, states or cities, and interests. The AI looks up the real options on Meta for you.

💰

Budget

A daily amount for each ad set, in naira. You choose it, not the AI.

🧪

Ad sets

Try a few audiences side by side to see which one works, such as one interest and one broad.

Try sayingPlan a campaign for my Herbal Tea: women 25 to 45 in Lagos and Ibadan, ₦5,000 a day, 2 ad sets. Show me the plan but do not launch.
Lesson 6 of 9

Make the ad pictures.

A good ad picture stops the scroll: a real person in the situation, your real product large, a few short benefit points and a clear call to action. The AI can make the picture, and it checks that your product pack looks right before you use it.

  • Pick the style, or show it two or three of your old winners to copy the look.
  • Keep the words on the picture short, so they stay spelled correctly.
  • Pictures stay as previews until you approve them. Then they are saved for that product.
Try sayingMake 3 ad pictures for my Herbal Tea. Use my product photo so the pack looks real.
Your product photo is not an ad pictureThe product photo is what customers see when they order. Ads use separate pictures made for ads.
Lesson 7 of 9

Write the ad words.

Revora writes long, hooky copy in the way your customers talk, including Pidgin or Yoruba if you like. It learns from your own best ads and uses only the facts saved on your product.

  • Ask for several versions and pick your favourites.
  • Revora keeps to Meta's rules: no guaranteed results, no before-and-after claims, no cures.
  • Save your best copy to your library and reuse it on any ad account.
Try sayingWrite 4 versions of ad copy for my Herbal Tea in simple English with a little Pidgin.
Lesson 8 of 9

Review, approve and launch.

The AI shows you the whole plan in plain words: who sees the ad, the budget, the pictures, the words and the rule that decides when a bad ad is stopped. You read it, ask for changes and only then say yes.

  • Approving turns the ads on right away and starts spending your money.
  • A kill rule flags ads that cost too much per result. Winners are never stopped.
  • You can pause or change a budget any time, by chat or in the dashboard.
Try sayingThe plan looks good. Launch it.
Lesson 9 of 9

Fund your ad account.

Ads run on the balance in your Meta ad account. Revora helps you top it up safely, and checks your balance live from Meta.

  • Media buyers ask for funding, and the owner or an admin approves it.
  • The approved amount is sent to the Meta account's bank-transfer details, and nothing else is ever accepted.
  • Every transfer needs your own PIN, typed into a private form that the AI never sees. You get a receipt each time.
Try sayingWhat is the balance on my ad accounts?
