Why ad accounts get restricted, and how to protect yours

Losing an ad account in the middle of a good campaign hurts. Most restrictions come from a few avoidable habits. Meta's rules change, so always read their current advertising policies too.

Common causes

  • Promises that are too big. Guaranteed results, miracle claims or "before and after" claims about health.
  • Pictures or words about personal traits, like talking to people about their body or health problems.
  • Payment problems. A failed or declined payment can pause delivery.
  • Sudden big changes. Doubling the budget overnight or logging in from many places.
  • Lots of complaints. If people keep reporting your ads, your account suffers.

Habits that protect you

  • Keep your claims honest and specific.
  • Use your own domain with a clear business page, a privacy policy and contact details.
  • Increase budgets in steady steps.
  • Keep your payment method funded.
  • Answer comments and customer messages politely.

Do not put all your ads in one account

If you run ads for several products or clients, spread the risk and keep each account clean.
